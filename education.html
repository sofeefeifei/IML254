Sekolah Rendah Kebangsaan Jalan U3

My educational journey began at Sekolah Kebangsaan Jalan U3, where I completed both my preschool and primary education until I was 9 years old.
These early years played an important role in shaping my character, values, and passion for learning. It was during this time that I developed fundamental academic skills and gained many memorable experiences that contributed to my personal growth.

Sekolah Kebangsaan Subang Bestari

I continued my studies at Sekolah Kebangsaan Subang Bestari. This marked a new chapter in my educational journey, where I had the opportunity to adapt to a new environment, meet new friends, and gain new experiences.
My years at Sekolah Kebangsaan Subang Bestari were filled with valuable learning opportunities and memorable moments. The school helped me strengthen my academic knowledge while also developing important qualities such as responsibility, confidence and teamwork.

Sekolah Menengah Kebangsaan Subang Bestari

After completing my primary education, I continued my studies at SMK Subang Bestari. My secondary school years were an important period of growth, learning and self-discovery. During this time, I not only expanded my academic knowledge but also developed valuable life skills that helped shape my personality and future goals.
At SMK Subang Bestari, I had the opportunity to meet people from different backgrounds, build lasting friendships, and participate in various school activities. These experiences taught me the importance of teamwork, communication, and perseverance.

UiTM Cawangan Kedah

Currently, I am pursuing a Diploma in Library Informatics at UiTM Cawangan Kedah. Entering university marked a significant milestone in my educational journey, as it allowed me to further develop my knowledge, skills and interests in the field of information and library management.
Life at UiTM Cawangan Kedah has also provided me with opportunities to meet new people, build meaningful friendships and participate in academic and co-curricular activities. Being away from home has taught me to become more independent, responsible and adaptable in managing my studies and daily life.
